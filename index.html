Privacy Policy — Daily Space History

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Daily Space History ("we," "our") operates the @dailyspacehistory Instagram account, its companion Facebook Page, and the @dailyspacehistory YouTube channel. This app uses the Meta Graph API solely to publish content to our own Instagram and Facebook accounts and to view basic engagement metrics (likes, views, comments) on our own posts.

What we access: Our own Instagram Business account's posting and insights data via Meta's Graph API, using Standard Access. We do not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other Instagram or Facebook user or account.

YouTube: We use the YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Data API with read-only access to view analytics for our own YouTube channel (@dailyspacehistory). The app cannot upload, edit or delete anything. This data is stored only on the channel owner's computer and is never shared or sold. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do: We do not collect personal data from visitors, followers, or any third party. We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

Contact: For questions about this policy, contact bananarivermedia@gmail.com.
